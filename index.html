How Multiplayer Works (2–4 Players)
EASY SETUP

One player hosts the city and everyone else joins with an invite link. Each player controls their own Boss and fights for turf.

1 · Host the game

Pick Host Multiplayer, enter a Boss alias and press Create Room. Choose the map, speed and income, then press Launch Turf War when everyone is in.

2 · Invite your friends

Press Copy Invitation and send the link in chat. Friends can open it straight away, or paste it into the Join screen.

3 · Join a game

Pick Join Multiplayer, enter a Boss alias, paste the invite link and press Connect. Wait in the lobby until the host launches.

  • • Empty seats are filled by AI, so you can start with fewer friends.
  • • The host is the referee: their map and match settings are used for everyone.
  • • Keep the host's tab visible. Background tabs slow the game down.
  • • Everyone needs the same game version to connect.

Combat, Crews & Turf FIELD MANUAL
Combat & Controls
🕹️ Movement & Navigation

Use WASD / Arrows, Left-Click any street, or click the City Radar minimap to move.

⚡ Tactical Sprint (Shift)

Hold Shift while moving for a 1.6× speed burst up to 2.0s, followed by a 4.0s cooldown.

🔪 Switchblade Slash (Space / F)

Close-range cleave (52px reach, 20–40 dmg) hitting up to 3 enemies in front of you.

🔫 9mm Pistol (Right-Click)

Fires up to 1000px (40–80 dmg). Point-blank deals -50% (<100px) or -75% (≤50px); miss chance scales at 250px (5%), 500px (10%), 750px (25%), and 900px+ (50%).
